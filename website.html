Everything before the sample is cut

Four pieces, one pattern.

Each part of Nima is built around a real step in getting a garment made — not a generic feature list.

Piece 01

Flat sketching

Draft croquis and technical flats on a vector canvas built for garment shapes — darts, seams, and hardware included as real objects.

Piece 02

Tech packs

Every flat generates its own spec sheet automatically — measurements, seam allowance, and construction notes, ready to send to a maker.

Piece 03

Fabric library

Keep swatches, supplier contacts, fibre content, and minimums in one place, tagged to the styles they belong to.

Piece 04

Line sheets

Turn a collection into a clean, shareable sheet for buyers and press — pricing, colourways, and delivery dates included.
